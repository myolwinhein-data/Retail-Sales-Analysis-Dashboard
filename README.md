## 🔍 Project Overview

**Retail Sales Analysis** project transforms raw retail operational data into a powerful, executive-ready BI solution.The dashboard provides multi-layered visibility across five crucial business dimensions. It empowers stakeholders to track revenue growth, pinpoint high-value customer segments, evaluate regional profitability, and optimize product lines.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Preview & Sections

The dashboard features a clean, unified multi-page interface accessible via a persistent navigation panel:

1. **Executive Overview**
   - **Monthly Sales Trend:** Tracks revenue performance across months to identify peak selling seasons.
   - **Category Performance:** Highlights top-performing product categories (Furniture, Technology, Office Supplies).
   - **Regional Revenue:** Visualizes sales distribution by state using a treemap view.
   - **Financial Health:** Combines monthly sales, profit, and profit margin trends in a single view.

2. **Sales Performance**
   - **Year-over-Year (YoY) Comparison:** Compares current sales trends against previous year benchmarks.
   - **Sub-Category Rankings:** Ranks top 5 sub-categories by total sales volume.
   - **Fulfillment Channels:** Analyzes customer preference across shipping methods (Standard Class, Second Class, First Class, Same Day).
   - **Month-over-Month (MoM) Growth:** Tracks short-term sales momentum against prior months.

3. **Profitability Analysis**
   - **Profit Trajectory:** Monitors monthly profit growth and stability over time.
   - **Segment Profit Contribution:** Compares profit generated by Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
   - **Geographic Profitability:** Identifies top profit-generating states (New York, California, Washington, etc.).
   - **Discount Impact Analysis:** Evaluates how discount levels affect net profitability using a scatter plot.

4. **Customer Analysis**
   - **Order Frequency:** Tracks how often repeat customers place orders.
   - **Top Customers by Sales:** Identifies the highest-value individual customers.
   - **Customer Segment Share:** Displays the percentage breakdown across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
   - **Customer Performance Table:** Detailed table showing total orders, spend, and quantity per customer and region.

5. **Product Analysis**
   - **Top Frequency Products:** Highlights products with the highest order counts.
   - **Top Revenue Products:** Ranks products generating the highest gross dollar sales.
   - **Volume Sold:** Ranks items by total units sold.
   - **Profit vs. Quantity Relationship:** Scatter plot mapping unit volume against net profit to identify high-margin winners versus loss leaders.

## 💡 Key Analytical Insights

* **Category Dominance:** Furniture ($535.4K) and Technology ($519.2K) are the primary revenue drivers, outperforming Office Supplies ($427.3K).
* **Fulfillment Preferences:** Standard Class shipping accounts for the overwhelming majority ($1.4M) of order fulfillment, while Same Day delivery represents the smallest portion ($0.1M).
* **Customer Segment Breakdown:** The Consumer segment is the single largest driver of business volume, generating **51.58%** of orders (409 customers) and **$55K** in total profit, outperforming Corporate (29.76%) and Home Office (18.66%).
* **Top Performers & Accounts:** Individual top accounts like **Sean Miller ($25K)** and **Tamara Chand ($19K)** drive high revenue, while items like **Staples** and **Staple envelopes** represent the highest repeat order volume.
* **Regional Profit Leaders:** **California** and **New York** generate the highest overall revenues and net profitability across all geographic regions.
* **Discount Impact:** Scatter plot evaluation shows that higher discount levels severely erode net margins, resulting in negative profitability on select high-volume products.
